# What I'm doing now - March 2026

## South Africa, Mozambique, Zimbabwe 

I am in Harare, the capital of Zimbabwe right now. After my [keynote at Adopting Bitcoin in Cape Town](https://anita.onl/opt-out) I traveled to [Maputo in Mozambique to visit local communities and conduct workshops](https://bffbtc.org/mozambique-2026/) with Bitcoin Famba.

We are working on a video documenting the Mozambique tour and a report for our donors. Over the last couple of months we spent a lot of time on setting up a new fundraising structure that allows Bitcoin for Fairness to accept donations in USD (through a 501c3 entity), not only bitcoin.

A lot of time goes into educating myself about [using AI tools](/ai) to understand capabilities and limitations and how I want to use these tools in my work. In April I will update my (L)earn Bitcoin book - with the help of LLMs - and prepare my talk for BTC++ in Vienna.

If you want to [support the mission of Bitcoin for Fairness feel free to donate](https://bffbtc.org/donate).
