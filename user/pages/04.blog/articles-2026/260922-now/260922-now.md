# What I'm doing now - September 2026

## Looking Back to Move Forward

Since I founded [Bitcoin for Fairness](https://bffbtc.org) in late 2021, traveling and working have been intensive and taxing on my health. I took some time over the summer to look back on what we achieved in recent years and what I want to focus on in the future.

We closed the [Crack the Orange](https://my.cracktheorange.com/scholarship) scholarship program, which was a huge success with 156 graduates from 20 African countries, all Bitcoin builders and educators, many of whom started their own initiatives in their region. Currently, we are writing a report for our donors and will update the BFF website accordingly.

I am spending a lot of my time learning about nutrition and doing sports. I started serious weight training in July as I understand the importance of it for a long, healthy life. Additionally, I get my basic endurance back with long and rather boring bike rides.

Reflecting on my journey led me to the decision to find a permanent apartment again. I will be in South Africa from December to March, which means the search will start in 2027.
